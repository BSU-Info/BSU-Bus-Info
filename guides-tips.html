Created by walix2
Helpful tips to help you progress faster in Bus Simulator: Ultimate.

1️⃣ Best First Bus

⭐ Recommendation: Setra TopClass S 431 DT (175K Coins)

The Setra TopClass S 431 DT is one of the best first buses to buy because it gives excellent value for its price.

  • 👥 73 passenger capacity.
  • ⭐ Maximum comfort rating.
  • 🚻 Includes WC feature.
  • 🚌 Luxury double-decker coach.
  • 💰 Great balance between price, comfort and earnings.

2️⃣ Play Online Routes

Online Routes are a great way to experience more of BSU.

  • 🌍 Drive with other players.
  • 🛣️ Access more routes than default maps.
  • 💰 Earn more coins through more trips.
  • 🎮 Makes the game more realistic and fun.

3️⃣ Spend Your Coins Wisely

Don't waste coins on buses with low capacity or poor comfort.

  • 👥 Prioritise higher passenger capacity.
  • ⭐ Choose buses with better comfort ratings.
  • 💰 Buy buses with good value for money.
  • 📈 Smart purchases help you grow your company faster.

4️⃣ Drive Smoothly & Keep Passengers Happy

Good driving keeps passengers satisfied and improves your trips.

  • 🛣️ Drive smoothly on roads and turns.
  • 🚦 Avoid going above 127 km/h.
  • 🎛️ Cruise Control around 120 km/h is recommended.
  • 🚏 Stop at bus stops when needed.
  • 🍔 Use Catering to serve passengers.

5️⃣ Manage Your Fuel

Always prepare before long journeys.

  • ⛽ Check fuel before starting trips.
  • 🚏 Refuel when fuel reaches 40% or below.
  • 🚫 Running out of fuel affects passengers.
  • 🔧 Automatic refuelling works but lowers satisfaction.
  • 📈 Refuelling early keeps trips smooth.
