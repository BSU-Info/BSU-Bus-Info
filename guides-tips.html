📖 BSU Guides & Tips

Created by walix2
Helpful tips to help you progress faster in Bus Simulator: Ultimate.

1️⃣ Best First Bus

⭐ Recommendation: Setra TopClass S 431 DT (175K Coins)

The Setra TopClass S 431 DT is one of the best buses to buy when you have 175K Coins. It offers excellent value for money and can help you progress through the game faster.

  • 👥 73-passenger capacity.
  • ⭐ Maximum Comfort Rating.
  • 🚻 Includes a WC (Toilet) feature.
  • 🚌 Luxury double-decker coach.
  • 💰 Excellent balance of price, comfort and earning potential.

2️⃣ Play Online Routes

Instead of only using the default routes, try Online Routes.

  • 🌍 Join multiplayer servers and drive with other players.
  • 🛣️ Access many more routes than the default UK map.
  • 💰 More routes mean more earning opportunities and more coins.
  • 🎮 Multiplayer makes the game more fun and immersive.
  • 🚌 You aren't limited to the two default UK routes.

3️⃣ Spend Your Coins Wisely

Don't spend your coins on buses with low passenger capacity or poor comfort ratings.

  • 👥 Prioritise buses with higher passenger capacity.
  • ⭐ Choose buses with good comfort ratings.
  • 💰 Focus on value buses that offer the best balance of price, capacity, comfort and features.
  • 📈 Smart purchases help you earn coins faster.

Once you've built a fleet of good value buses and have a steady income, you're free to buy any buses you like for their looks or personal preference! 🚍✨

4️⃣ Drive Smoothly & Keep Passengers Happy

Driving carefully is one of the best ways to maintain high passenger satisfaction.

  • 🛣️ Drive smoothly, especially when exiting onto side roads.
  • 🚦 Avoid exceeding 127 km/h or passengers may complain.
  • 🎛️ Cruise Control at around 120 km/h is recommended.
  • 🚌 Stop at bus stops when your passengers' hunger level is low.
  • 🍔 Use the Catering option to serve food and drinks directly on the bus.

5️⃣ Manage Your Fuel

Always keep an eye on your fuel level, especially before starting a long trip.

  • ⛽ Make sure you have enough fuel before long journeys.
  • 🚏 Refuel when your fuel level reaches 40% or below.
  • 🚫 Running out of fuel will leave your bus stranded.
  • 🔧 The game will automatically refuel your bus, but your passengers will become unsatisfied.
  • 📈 Refuelling early keeps your trips running smoothly and your passengers happy.
